Toward the back, the home opens to the terrace and garden patio, where landscape and architecture meet. The original well was preserved as a historical vestige, alongside the former rooftop water tank reinterpreted as a sculptural decorative element — blending memory with contemporary design. Upstairs, two bedrooms each feature their own walk-in closet, thoughtfully designed for privacy, airflow, and constant natural light. Casa verde was renovated with Méridas iconic indoor-outdoor lifestyle in mind. A home created to live and enjoy in a tranquil yet connected area of town.
